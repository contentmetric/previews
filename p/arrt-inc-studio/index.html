Message us

Message us

  • What ages do you teach?

    Jellybean classes are for ages 2.5 to 5, Regular classes for ages 6 to 12, Drawing classes for ages 9 and above, and ASD classes for teenagers and young adults aged 13 and above.

  • Can we try a class first?

    Yes. A trial class is SGD 40 for each class type, as listed on the studio’s booking page.

  • How do I book?

    Send a WhatsApp message to +65 8894 3352 with the class you are interested in.

  • Do you run holiday programmes?

    Yes. Holiday programmes run in the school holidays, with bundle packages. Message the studio on WhatsApp for the dates and details.

  • Do you offer 1-to-1 classes?

    Yes. The studio offers 1-to-1 classes and ASD classes. Message the studio to ask what suits your child.

  • Where is the studio?

    10 Woodlands Square, Woods Square, Solo 1, #02-45, Singapore 737714.

  • What are the opening hours?

    Tuesday to Friday 4pm to 8:30pm, Saturday and Sunday 9:30am to 7:30pm. Closed on Monday.

  • Are there classes for adults?

    Some reviewers describe adult classes at the studio. Message the studio to ask what is available now.
