Good to know

  

    
How far ahead should I order?
At least 1 week ahead. J & Q Bakery Xperts bakes to order and takes pre-orders only.

    
Do you deliver?
Yes, or you can collect. The delivery fee depends on your area, and J & Q confirms it on WhatsApp. Pick collect or delivery in the form above.

    
Can you work around an allergy?
Tap your allergy in the form and it goes into your message. If it is severe, say so on WhatsApp so J & Q can tell you what is safe to bake.

    
When can I reach you?
Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm. Closed on Saturday and Sunday. Messages sent outside those hours are answered when J & Q is back.
