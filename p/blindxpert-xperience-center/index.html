About us

premium shading specialists

Welcome to BlindXpert Xperience Center. We bring over a decade of expertise to indoor and outdoor shades for Singapore homes: blinds, curtains, insect screens, zip blinds and outdoor roofing. Everything is made to measure and professionally installed by an experienced local team, and our two showrooms let you see the materials, colours and operating systems before you decide. After-sales support stays with you once your shades are up.

Services

What we do

Five product lines for windows, balconies and outdoor spaces.

BX MultiPro Screen

Insect, pet and safety screen solutions for your windows and doors. The BX MultiPro Screen is built for protection, privacy and pet-friendly homes. It is made to measure for HDB, condominium and landed homes, installed by our own local team, and you can see it working at our North and East showrooms.

Outdoor Blinds

SmartZip track systems for balconies and outdoor openings, built for the Singapore climate. Choose from the SmartZip Flagship, Tiptronic 4-in-1 ZipBlind system and SmartZip elero ranges, plus Megaview (up to 12 m) and Higher View (up to 7 m) for larger and taller openings.

Indoor Blinds

Roller, zebra, venetian, honeycomb and roman blinds, made to measure and professionally installed for HDB, condominium and landed homes. Visit a showroom to compare fabrics, colours and operating systems side by side, and our team will recommend the right blind for each room.

Curtains

Curtains and Dream Curtain for living rooms and bedrooms, made to measure to suit your windows and your home. See the fabrics and colours in person at our showrooms, then our experienced local team measures, makes and installs them for you.

Outdoor Roofing

Outdoor roofing and shading for terraces and balconies: Smart-LUV aluminium louvre roofing, Skyline retractable roofing, trellis roofing, the motorised Smart Awning and the Smart-Pergo retractable pergola. Each one is measured for your space and installed by our team.

Smart Home

Smart home integration through Yubii Home. Ask us how your shades can fit in with the rest of your home. Our team can show you the options at our North and East showrooms, open every day from 10am to 7pm, including public holidays.

FAQs

Answering your questions

Got more questions? Send us a photo of your window

Message us on WhatsApp

Where can I see the products?

Visit our North showroom at 21 Woodland Close, #01-25, Prizm Bizhub, or our East showroom at 33 Ubi Ave 3, #01-30 Vertex. Both are open daily, 10am to 7pm.

Which homes do you serve?

We supply blinds, curtains, zip blinds, insect screens and outdoor roofing for HDB flats, condominiums and landed homes across Singapore. Our experienced local team measures and installs every product, so it is made to fit your home.

Is everything made to measure?

Yes. Everything we supply is made to measure, and an experienced local team measures and installs it professionally.

Which products do you offer?

Indoor blinds and curtains, outdoor zip blinds, the BX MultiPro insect, pet and safety screen, outdoor roofing and shading such as Skyline retractable roofing and the Smart-Pergo, and smart home integration through Yubii Home.

Do you offer after-sales support?

Yes. After-sales support is part of our service, so you can reach us after your shades are installed. Message us on WhatsApp or visit either showroom, open Monday to Sunday from 10am to 7pm.

What if I am not sure what suits my windows?

Send us a photo of your window on WhatsApp and our team will recommend a suitable solution. You can also visit a showroom to see the materials, colours and operating systems in person, and compare blinds, curtains and screens side by side before you decide what suits each room.

How do I get a quote?

Message us on WhatsApp with a photo of your window and what you have in mind. Our team will recommend a suitable solution and arrange the measuring, and we will take it from there.
