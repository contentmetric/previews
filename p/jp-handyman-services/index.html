// FAQ //

Questions, answered.

What services do you offer?

Plumbing, electrical, furniture assembly, wall mounting, doors and locks, painting, plastering, and hinge and drawer slider repairs, for homes and businesses across Singapore.

Do you give free quotations?

Yes. WhatsApp us the details and photos of the job and we give you a no-obligation quote before any work begins.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Singapore, islandwide: from Tampines and Bedok in the east to Jurong and Clementi in the west, Woodlands and Yishun in the north, and Punggol and Ang Mo Kio in the north-east.

Is there a transport fee?

Our price lists show a transport fee of $30. It covers bringing our tools and materials to your location.

How fast do you reply?

Most customers hear back within 30 minutes during business hours, Monday to Saturday 9am to 7pm. Urgent jobs can often be scheduled the same day.
