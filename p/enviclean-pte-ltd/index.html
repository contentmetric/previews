Our 7-step deep clean

The process we use on mattresses, fabric sofas and carpets, with an industrial-grade hot water extraction machine.

Mattress, sofa and carpet

  • 1Surface removal of dust, hairs and tiny particles
  • 2Shampoo treatment to lift stubborn stains and odour
  • 3170 degree water steaming against bacteria, germs and dust mites
  • 4Deep scrub, polishing with a machine to loosen and break stains
  • 5Hot water extraction of dead skin, flakes, dust mites and more
  • 6Deep vacuuming of excess water and remaining particles
  • 7Hospital-grade anti-bacterial disinfectant for you and your loved ones

Good to know

  • Queen mattressabout 25 to 30 min
  • 3-seater sofaabout 45 min
  • Drying timeabout 4 to 5 hours
  • Recommendedevery 4 to 6 months
  • With kids or petsevery 3 months

Timings vary with the size and condition of the item. Our cleaning agents are safe for children, pets and sensitive skin. We do our best to remove or lighten stains, though not every stain can be removed.
