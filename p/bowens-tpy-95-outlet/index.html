The Bowen’s Story

Bowen’s began on 2 April 2015 as Bowen’s Western in Pandan Gardens, then grew to Bowen’s Cafe at Margaret Market. Our Toa Payoh outlet brings the same Italian-inspired cooking closer to you, at prices that stay friendly.
