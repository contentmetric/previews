Got questions?

Straight answers to the questions homeowners ask us most.

What services does Home Clean Home offer?

We provide a complete, single-source solution for your home, including regular housekeeping, post-renovation cleaning, floor scrubbing, move-in/move-out handovers, and seasonal deep cleans. We also specialise in indoor air quality (IAQ) treatments, such as formaldehyde (VOC) removal, to keep your space spotlessly clean and your air fresh.

Are your cleaners licensed and certified?

Yes! Home Clean Home is an NEA-licensed cleaning provider in Singapore. We operate under strict ISO standards and have over a decade of hands-on expertise in professional house care and indoor air quality (IAQ) specialisation.

How do your cleaning packages work?

We offer simple fixed packages for regular housekeeping, move-in/move-out handovers, and seasonal deep cleans. You can also customise your service with flexible add-ons, like specialist formaldehyde removal or deep air-purifying treatments, to suit your home’s exact needs.

What makes your indoor air quality (IAQ) treatments different from standard cleaning?

While regular cleaning focuses on visible surfaces, our certified IAQ treatments target invisible indoor pollutants, including harmful formaldehyde and volatile organic compounds (VOCs). Using advanced technology, we ensure the air your family breathes is as healthy and fresh as your clean surfaces.

How do I book a cleaning package or request a quote?

Booking is quick and effortless! Simply select your preferred package or add-on services directly through WhatsApp, and we will help you find the right home and air care solution for your space.
