Questions, answered.

The things people ask us most before they book a clean.

How much does it cost?

Jobs start from $65, steaming included. A 3-seater sofa is $105, a queen mattress is $105 and an office chair is $45. We confirm the full price before we start.

How soon can you come?

Same-week slots are usually available. We respond to every request within 2 hours during operating hours, Monday to Saturday, 9am to 7pm.

Do you clean at my home?

Yes. We only clean on site, so nothing leaves your home or office, and we leave no mess behind.

Is it safe for kids and pets?

Yes. Safe for Kids and Pets is one of the things we promise, and we leave no mess behind.

Is there a discount?

New customers get 25% off the first piece, and every extra piece on the same visit is 40% off.

What can you clean?

Sofas, mattresses and beds, carpets and rugs, office and gaming chairs, baby prams, cots and car seats, dining chairs and curtains.
