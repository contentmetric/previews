Questions parents ask

Where are you?

Blk 806 Hougang Central, #03-144, Singapore 530806, about a 3 minute walk from Hougang MRT.

Which levels and subjects do you teach?

Chinese and Higher Chinese, from K1 to Secondary 4 (O Level), following the MOE syllabus. Our courses include Pinyin Beginner, Chinese Enrichment, Creative Writing & Comprehension and Secondary Chinese Enrichment.

Do you have Higher Chinese?

Yes. Our lessons follow the MOE Chinese and Higher Chinese syllabus and exam formats.

What if my child misses a lesson?

We arrange a make-up lesson within the same week.

How do I ask about fees and places?

Message us on WhatsApp at 9616 0836 with your child's level, and we will reply about places and fees.

My family doesn't speak Chinese at home. Can you help?

Yes. Parents from non-Chinese-speaking families write in their Google reviews that their children gained real confidence in both oral and written Chinese.
