Questions parents ask

Where are you?

1 Punggol Walk, Waterway Terraces, Singapore 821308, about a 2 minute walk from Punggol MRT.

Which levels and subjects do you teach?

Tamil from preschool to Secondary, English and Maths from kindergarten to Secondary, and Science from Primary 3 to Secondary 4, all following the MOE syllabus. We also run Speech and Drama and art workshops for all ages.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes. Our Google listing shows both on-site and online classes. Message us on WhatsApp to ask which suits your child.

Can my child try a lesson first?

Yes, we offer a trial class. Message us on WhatsApp with your child's level and subject.

How do I ask about fees and places?

Message us on WhatsApp at 9671 3729 with your child's level and subject, and we will reply about places and fees.

My family doesn't speak Tamil at home. Can you help?

Yes. Non-Tamil-speaking parents write in their Google reviews that the teachers are patient and make learning Tamil fun and accessible, and that their children have improved.
