LAMDA exams

Exams and the UCAS Tariff.

The Level 3 LAMDA exams (Grades 6, 7 and 8, the Bronze, Silver and Gold Medal exams) are recognised within the UCAS Tariff, the system of points for qualifications used for entry to higher education. It lets students use a range of qualifications to help secure a place on a course.

Assessment preparation

A programme built around the school and the child.

01

Mock assessments

Our unique programme uses mock assessments with several different tutors, to help students preparing for school interviews. It can also help children who find communication difficult, building conversational skills and self-confidence.

02

Detailed weekly feedback

We target each child’s own needs, then tutor and polish their skills so they can show their full potential. Parents receive detailed weekly feedback, and we help children feel at ease in their assessments.

03

Tailored to each school

Classes are bespoke to each school’s criteria. We focus on each child’s self-development, building world knowledge, spontaneous communication skills, confidence and a transferable skill set. Start with a trial session to speak to the tutor and discuss your objectives.
