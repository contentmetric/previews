Why Little Acorns?

Taught by professional tutors.

Little Acorns Theatre Academy was established in 2001 by Lauren Shannon, a TV child actor in the BBC programme Grange Hill, who trained on scholarship at the Mountview Theatre Academy in London. Our LAMDA tuition equips learners, whatever their age or aspirations, with skills that serve them throughout life.

Our teachers have worked in some of the top UK schools and drama schools, including Harrow, Eton, the Royal Central School of Speech and Drama, Emil Dale and Cambridge University. Little Acorns is a certified LAMDA school, and at Grades 6 to 8 students can gain UCAS points.
