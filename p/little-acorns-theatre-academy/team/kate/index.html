Teacher

Kate Dobson

Kate graduated from drama school with a First-Class BA (Hons) degree in Acting from the Manchester School of Theatre. Since then, she has worked extensively as a professional actor and enjoys sharing her knowledge and passion through tutoring. She has achieved outstanding examination results for Little Acorns and has successfully prepared students for entry into prestigious Hertfordshire and Middlesex schools, including Merchant Taylors, Haberdashers, Westminster Boys, and Holland Park.
