Teacher

Samantha Bingley

Samantha fell in love with performing at a young age and made her professional debut at the age of seven in Cat on a Hot Tin Roof (Lyric Theatre). Over the years that followed, she trained with the National Youth Music Theatre, The British Theatre Academy and received a First Class Honours Degree from Brunel University.

Since graduating, Samantha has continued her work in the Musical Theatre industry alongside another creative love: teaching. With over a decade of teaching experience, Samantha aims to bring the work ethic of the professional acting world into each and every lesson, create a safe space for young people to express themselves and build their confidence.

Acting credits include:

  • Madame de la Grande Bouché - Beauty and the Beast (London Palladium and UK Tour)
  • Hey! Christmas Tree (Minerva, Chichester Festival Theatre)
  • To Wong Foo (Hope Mill Theatre)
  • At Last it’s Summer (London Palladium)
  • Dumbledore - Voldemort and the Teenage Hogwarts Parody Musical (King’s Head)
  • Claire - Ascension of Mrs Leech workshop (Troubadour Theatre)
  • Finalist - Stephen Sondheim Student Performer of the Year (Theatre Royal Haymarket)
  • Martha - The Secret Garden (Ambassador’s Theatre)
  • Susan - Prodigy (The Other Palace)

Teaching credits include:

  • LAMDA teacher - Little Acorns Theatre Academy
  • Visiting Lecturer (Musical Theatre History and Practice) - Brunel University
  • Musical Theatre Teacher - British Theatre Academy
  • Musical Theatre Teacher - PQA
