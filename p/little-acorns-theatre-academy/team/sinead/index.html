Teacher

Sinéad Wall

Sinéad Wall is an actor and singer from Leicester who trained at The London School of Musical Theatre and De Montfort Ubiversity, she is also an Associate of The London College of Music. Mainly working in musical theatre and live performance Sinéad was recently seen in Galaxy Train at The Other Palace Theatre and before that as the Special Guest Vocalist on the UK Tour of the Eric and Little Ern show. Her credits include Medusa in MonstersongsUK Premiere (The Other Palace) 1446 the Musical (The Other Palace). Yanna/Alex in Paper Hearts First Stage Theatre Hamburg Germany, Upstairs at The Gatehouse, London, Underbelly Edinburgh Fringe Festival. Barb in Romance Romance (Landor Theatre).

Sinead has also been a lead vocalist for AIDA cruise lines and has spent most Christmases playing the Villain in various pantomimes across the country. Sinéad is Head of Acting at Leicester College of Performing Arts and works in a variety of stage schools across London. Mainly working as a drama teacher, LAMDA acting coach and Director she has recently directed projects for students at Drama Schools Mountview Academy of Theatre Arts, Goldsmiths University of the Arts and recently directed a project for International Students at The Edinburgh Fringe Festival.
