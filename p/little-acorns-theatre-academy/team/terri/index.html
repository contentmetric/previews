Teacher

Terri Claus

Terri's journey from Glasgow, Scotland, to the epicenters of performance arts is nothing short of extraordinary. Armed with a passion for the stage, she embarked on a transformative educational odyssey, culminating in prestigious institutions such as the Glasgow Academy of Musical Theatre Arts, the Liverpool Institute for Performing Arts (founded by Sir Paul McCartney), and The Royal Academy of Music in London. Her stellar training laid the foundation for an illustrious career that has seen her grace renowned stages worldwide. From enchanting audiences as a vocalist with Elton John at iconic venues like the London Palladium and The Royal Albert Hall, to captivating performances in productions such as "A Christmas Carol" and "The Sound of Music," Terri's talent knows no bounds.

But her journey didn't end in the spotlight; driven by a desire to nurture the next generation of performers, she transitioned into teaching and directing. Over the years, Terri has become synonymous with children's creativity, collaborating with industry stalwarts and fostering young talent through her roles as a director, casting intern, and acting tutor at esteemed institutions across the globe. With an unwavering dedication to her craft, Terri continues to inspire and shape the future of the performing arts landscape.

Her creative credits include : Asst. Director (Kids of the West End, Leicester Square), Directing and Casting Intern (Mary Poppins UK Tour), Director’s Asst. (Sound of Music Intl.)

Her teaching roles include : Acting Teacher (Spirit Young Performers Company, London), Acting Tutor (Emil Dale Academy, Hitchin), Acting Tutor (Dang Academy, London), Shakespeare/Acting Coach (West End Prep, Newcastle), LAMDA Teacher (Princess Helena College, Hitchin), Acting/MT Teacher (Redroofs Theatre School, Maidenhead), LAMDA Teacher (Little Acorns Theatre Academy), Trinity MT/Acting Teacher (Susi Earnshaw Theatre School, London), BTEC Acting Teacher (Bodens Performing Arts, London), LAMDA/Singing Teacher (Innovation Dance Studios, London), Musical Theatre Coach (Miracle Works Studios, Seoul).
