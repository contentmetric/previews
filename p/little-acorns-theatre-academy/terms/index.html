Terms and Conditions.

Little Acorns Grow into Mighty Oaks. By accepting your time slot you are agreeing to the following terms and conditions for Little Acorns LAMDA sessions.

Our terms and conditions are to protect the tutors whose allocated time slots are pre-booked termly, in addition to the commitment of the hall rental, insurance costs and other overheads of running the classes.

Payment and cancellation

  1. Full payment for each half term is due on the first lesson.

  2. A terms notice must be given in writing. Any lessons you do not attend in this notice period will still be charged.

  3. Any lessons missed will still be charged, as your child’s teacher has already been booked, and the overheads of the session remain the same. If a lesson is cancelled by Little Acorns Theatre Academy, you will be refunded for this session.

  4. If payment is not received within 7 days of your first session back, an admin fee of £15.00 will be charged.

Sessions and examinations

  1. Students must commit to learning designated pieces in their own time in order to benefit fully from the sessions. No responsibility will be held by the tutor if a student does not fully prepare in their own time. Advice and guidance will be offered at all stages.

  2. Cancelling or postponing an examination may result in additional charges as set out in LAMDA’s terms and conditions. Any additional charges incurred will be charged to you.

  3. Exam dates will be discussed with the student or parent at the earliest opportunity, as availability with LAMDA may be limited. Exam dates must be kept completely free, as LAMDA will determine time slots.

  4. It is the student’s responsibility to make his or her own way to the designated exam venue. Lateness may result in the student missing the exam and therefore incurring additional fees to book a second date.

  5. Exam fees must be paid to Little Acorns before your child is entered for the relevant exam. Any late payment may result in your child not being entered.
