Good to know

Questions

How do I book?

Choose your items in the order builder and press send. WhatsApp opens with your order written out. We reply to confirm.

Where is the stall and when is it open?

Blk 29 Bendemeer Rd, #01-20, Bendemeer Hawker Centre, Singapore 330029. Open 6:30 am to 2 pm, Saturday to Thursday, and closed on Fridays.

Pick up or delivery?

Tell us which you would like when you send your order. We confirm the details, and any delivery fee, on WhatsApp.

What are the weekly specials?

A rotating list that includes chicken rice sets, chap chai and fragrant chicken rice. It changes with the week and with promotions, so ask us what is available on your day.

How do I pay?

We tell you how to pay when we confirm your order on WhatsApp. Nothing is charged on this page.

How many in a pack?

The packed nasi lemak is sold as a set of 10 packets, chicken or fish, at $42.90 a set.
