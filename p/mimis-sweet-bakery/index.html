Good to know

Questions

Why only once a month?

Mimi's is a one-woman home bakery. Baking everything in one go means every batch is fresh, nothing is wasted and prices stay fair.

How do I pay?

By PayNow, after your order is confirmed on WhatsApp. Your slot is secured once payment is received.

Are there any discounts?

Your first order is 10% off. Tick the box when you order. Planning a corporate or bulk order? Message us for a code. Discounts apply to your bakes, not delivery, and can't be combined.

When exactly will I get my brownies?

In the delivery week shown at the top of this page. The two hand-over days for the month are sent to you once presale closes.

How long do brownies keep?

Three days at room temperature in the box, a week in the fridge, or a month in the freezer. Cream cheese brownies are best kept chilled.

Can I change my order?

Yes, any time before presale closes on the 14th. After that the ingredients are already bought.

Allergens

All our bakes contain wheat, eggs and dairy. We don't add nuts to any bake unless it says so on that item, and anything with nuts will be clearly labelled.

Are your brownies halal?

Mimi's Sweet Bakery is not halal-certified. No alcohol and no lard are used in any of our bakes.
