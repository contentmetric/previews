// FAQ //

Questions, answered.

What jobs do you take?

Electrical (power points, switches, fans, water heater switches), plumbing (taps, toilet bowls, bidets, leaks), carpentry (repairs, shelving, cabinets, TV consoles) and fittings (door locks, picture hanging, curtains, baby gates, fencing).

Do you work on shops, gyms and other businesses?

Yes. We work for commercial and domestic customers. Past jobs include re-doing a whole gym floor and fixing a restaurant door that would not close.

Can you do bigger renovations?

Yes. We go from simple repairs to refurbishment, including a new kitchen, a new bathroom and a full re-decoration. We have also built TV consoles, feature walls and built-in cabinets.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday 9am to 5:30pm, Saturday 9am to 1pm. We are closed on Sunday.

How do I get a quote?

WhatsApp us a photo of the job on +65 8769 2169 and tell us what needs doing. We will give you a quote.
