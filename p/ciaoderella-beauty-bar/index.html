New treatment

New: Diode hair removal.

A diode hair removal machine uses a diode laser to send light energy into the hair. The pigment in the hair absorbs that light and turns it to heat, which warms the follicle and slows regrowth. The aim is long-lasting hair reduction.

Areas you can ask about

  • Underarms
  • Arms
  • Legs
  • Bikini line
  • Face and upper lip
  • Back

These are areas people commonly ask about, not a fixed menu. Tell us the area you have in mind and we will reply on WhatsApp.

  • Built up over a series of sessions

    Hair grows in cycles, and the light only works on hair in the right stage. So results build over several sessions spaced weeks apart, rather than in one visit.

  • Quick for small areas

    A small area such as the underarms or upper lip takes only a short time.
