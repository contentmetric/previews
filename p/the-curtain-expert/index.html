Before you buy

    
What people usually ask first.

    
How much do curtains and blinds cost in Singapore?
It depends on the fabric, window size and the system you choose. Curtains are usually priced by track length and fabric grade, while blinds are priced per window, so the fairest way to price your home is an on-site measurement followed by a clear, itemised quote. We also offer whole-home packages suited to HDB BTOs and condos. Get in touch for a no-obligation quote based on your actual windows.

    
Do you provide on-site measurement and installation?
Yes. Our consultant comes to your home to measure each window and recommend the right fabric, colour and system for how you use the room. Our own team then handles the full installation, so everything is mounted cleanly and hangs the way it should from day one. We serve homes and offices across Singapore.

    
Can I see fabric samples before ordering?
Yes. You are welcome to visit our showroom to see and feel the full range of curtain and blind fabrics in person. Our team is on hand to guide you, and our consultant can also bring samples along to your on-site measurement.
