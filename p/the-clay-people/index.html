Create, learn and share clay art.

The Clay People is a creative space that welcomes ceramic enthusiasts from all walks of life to bring their ideas to life in clay.

The studio sits in Toa Payoh Industrial Park, among the crafting and production workshops that give the area its quiet, nostalgic feel.

What you’ll learn

Hand-building, wheel throwing and glazing, with patient, beginner-friendly guidance from the studio.

Teo Boon Gim

Head ceramist, The Clay People

Teo Boon Gim runs the studio. He is a veteran in wheel throwing and experienced in ceramic sculptural art, and he began training locally under the apprenticeship of renowned masters in Asia.

Still have a question?

Message us

Message us

  • Do I need pottery experience?

    No. The Muddy Hands trial is made for first-timers, and the introductory courses are for anyone starting out with clay.

  • How do I book?

    Send a WhatsApp message to +65 9692 8030 with the class you are interested in.

  • How much do classes cost?

    Fees depend on the class and can change, so please message the studio on WhatsApp for the current rates.

  • Where is the studio?

    1003 Toa Payoh Industrial Park, #07-1527, Singapore 319075, beside the CTE and PIE expressways. The nearest MRT stations are Toa Payoh and Potong Pasir.

  • What are the opening hours?

    Monday to Friday 11am to 10pm, Saturday and Sunday 9am to 10pm.

  • Can I bring a team or a group?

    Yes. The studio runs team-bonding workshops for companies, friends and families.

  • Do you run workshops for schools?

    Yes. The studio runs workshops in schools as well as classes for adult learners at the studio.

  • Do you take commissions?

    Yes. The studio makes custom pottery pieces tailored to your vision, and also offers glazing and firing services.
