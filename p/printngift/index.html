// FAQ //

Good to know.

What is the minimum order?

50 pieces for printing your logo on our ready-stock gifts. Fully customised gifts start at 500 pieces. Bulk discounts begin from 500 pieces.

How long does production take?

Ready-stock gifts with your logo take about 7 to 10 business days. Fully customised gifts take about 10 to 15 business days for a sample and 30 to 45 for mass production. It depends on stock, artwork approval and payment.

Can you deliver overseas?

Yes. Besides door-to-door delivery and bulk delivery to multiple locations in Singapore, we ship internationally to overseas offices and clients.

Can you make a fully customised gift?

Yes. Fully customised gifts need product development, so an initial prototype carries a sample development fee. Chat with us and we will advise.

Do you store and send gifts for us?

Yes. We offer warehousing, distribution and an online gift tracking and inventory system, from our Tai Seng warehouse.
