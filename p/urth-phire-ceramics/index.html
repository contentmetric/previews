Earth and fire, on the wheel.

Urth&Phire is a ceramic studio founded in 2017 by artist Alvin Leow. The name is a play on earth and fire: raw clay meeting the kiln.

Classes are small and led by practising ceramic artists, from a first trial session to advanced forms.

From first trial to advanced forms

Wheel throwing taught in small groups, one step at a time. Every student takes home at least one glaze-fired piece.

Still have a question?

Message us

Message us

  • Do I need pottery experience?

    No. The trial class and beginner course are designed for complete beginners, and the instructors guide you step by step.

  • What will I bring home?

    Every student takes home at least one glaze-fired piece. Pieces are glazed and fired by the studio team and are ready to collect in about 3 to 4 weeks.

  • How much do classes cost?

    The trial class is S$60, the six-session beginner course is S$380 and the open studio monthly pass is S$200. Prices are as listed on the studio’s website and may change.

  • How big are the classes?

    Small. The beginner course runs with groups of three students per instructor.

  • Where is the studio?

    80 Playfair Road #04-01, Kapo Factory Building, Block A, Singapore 367998, a 3-minute walk from Tai Seng MRT.

  • What are the opening hours?

    The studio is open daily and closed on Thursdays.

  • Can I book a team or company workshop?

    Yes. The studio runs corporate and team-building pottery workshops tailored to groups.

  • Do you take commissions?

    Yes. The studio welcomes bespoke ceramic commissions for individuals, brands and businesses, from custom tableware to art pieces.
