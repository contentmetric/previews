Aesthetic clinic

Commercial fit-out

Project overview

This aesthetic clinic needed storage that looks clean and holds a lot. The photos show tall cabinets, rows of drawers and a built-in vanity with a basin, all in one palette.

The tall cabinets are finished in oak laminate. The drawers are grey-green, and dark frames outline parts of the joinery. Together they give the rooms a calm, consistent look.

In a clinic, a late or repeated visit means a room out of use. So the carpentry has to fit properly the first time.

What the photos show

  • Tall oak-laminate cabinets: Full-height units use the wall from floor to ceiling, so storage is larger and there is no dusty gap on top.

  • Grey-green drawers: A row of drawers in a soft grey-green gives easy access to supplies and breaks up the oak.

  • Built-in vanity with basin: The vanity is part of the joinery, with the basin set into the top and the cabinet shaped around it.

  • Dark frames: Dark outlines frame parts of the cabinetry and give the design clear edges.

  • One consistent finish: The same materials run through the rooms, so the clinic reads as a single space.

How we made it fit

We measure the walls on site, draw each cabinet and cut every part to the drawing. For a full-height unit, that means the cabinet meets the ceiling and the side wall with even edges. For the vanity, we confirm the basin and its cut-out before we cut.

On every install we check doors, drawers and gaps before we hand over. If you are planning a clinic, send us the floor plan and a few photos on WhatsApp.
