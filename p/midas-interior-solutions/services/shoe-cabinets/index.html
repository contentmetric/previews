Shoe & storage cabinets

Homes

A shoe cabinet for your entrance

The entrance is where shoes, bags, umbrellas and keys pile up. A ready-made shoe cabinet rarely matches the space. It leaves a gap at the side, stops short of the ceiling or blocks the door.

Midas Interior Solutions is a direct carpentry workshop. We measure your foyer, draw the cabinet, cut every part to the drawing on our own CNC machine in our own Woodlands workshop, and install it ourselves. No middleman, and nothing subcontracted. If you are looking for a shoe cabinet in Singapore that fits your own entrance, we make it to your wall.

What we make

Entryway storage can be slim or full-height, open or closed.

  • Full-height shoe cabinets: floor to ceiling, with tilt-out or drawer compartments for pairs of shoes.

  • Slim shoe cabinets: shallow units made for narrow HDB corridors.

  • Bench and shoe storage: a seat for putting on shoes, with storage beneath.

  • Storage walls: shoes, coats, bags, a vacuum cleaner and household items behind one set of doors.

  • Open niches and shelves: a place for keys, mail and a plant at the door.

  • Mirrors and lit niches: a full-length mirror door or an LED strip for a clear view on the way out.

How we make sure it fits

Entrances are tight. The cabinet has to clear the front door, the gate, the distribution board and often a corner wall. We plan around each of these.

Door swing: we measure the front door and gate and check that both open fully, with the cabinet in place.

Depth: a tilt-out compartment needs enough depth to swing. We check the corridor width and set the cabinet depth so you can still walk past.

Uneven walls: older flats have walls that lean. We measure at several heights and add a scribe strip so the face stays straight.

Fixed items: the DB box, meter, switches and air vents are marked on the drawing, and cut-outs are made to those marks on our CNC machine.

Materials and humidity

Shoes bring in moisture and grit, and the entrance is often next to the corridor or the open gate. Singapore humidity of 70 to 90 per cent adds to the load.

We choose the board for the position, seal every cut edge with edge banding and raise the base off the floor so mopping does not wet the wood. We can add ventilation slots or a perforated panel to let the air move, which helps with the smell. Laminate and veneer finishes are options, and your quote lists the board, finish and hardware.

Planning what goes inside

The inside matters as much as the outside. We ask how many pairs you own, how tall your boots are and who uses the cabinet.

Adjustable shelves suit a mix of heels, trainers and sandals. Tilt-out compartments hold more pairs in a shallow space. Tall slots take boots and umbrellas. A drawer at hand height holds keys, masks and loose items. Tell us your needs and we draw the layout to match.

From measurement to installation

Five steps, with the same team throughout.

  1. Send us a photo: share your entrance on WhatsApp and tell us how many pairs you want to store.

  2. Measure on site: we measure the wall, ceiling, door swing and fixed items, after the wet works are done.

  3. Drawing: we draw the cabinet to scale with the internal layout, and you confirm it before we cut.

  4. Make: each part is cut to the drawing on our own CNC machine and finished in our own workshop. Nothing is sent out.

  5. Install and walk-through: we fit the cabinet, adjust the doors and compartments and go through it with you.

What to prepare before we measure

A few things on your side help us get the numbers right.

  • Finish plastering and tiling: the floor and wall surface should be final before we measure.

  • Clear the entrance: we need to reach the floor, the ceiling and the side walls.

  • Count your shoes: it decides the cabinet width and the compartment type.

  • Point out fixed items: the DB box, vents, switches and the gate swing.

  • Tell us who uses it: children, elderly family members or guests need different heights.

Common questions
Can a shoe cabinet fit a narrow HDB corridor?

Yes. We make slim units with a shallow depth, and we check the door and gate swing on site before we draw.

How many pairs of shoes can a cabinet hold?

It depends on the size and the compartment type. Tilt-out compartments hold more in a shallow space. We plan the layout from the number of pairs you own.

How do you stop the cabinet from smelling?

We can add ventilation slots or a perforated panel so air moves through the cabinet, and we keep the base off the floor.

Will the cabinet block the DB box or the gate?

No. We mark the DB box, vents and gate swing on the drawing and plan the cabinet around them.

Can I add a bench or a mirror?

Yes. We can add a seat with storage below, a full-length mirror door or a lit niche.
