Platform beds

Bedrooms

A platform bed made for your bedroom

Many Singapore bedrooms are small. A standard bed frame leaves dead space at the sides and under the mattress, and it may not leave room for the wardrobe door to open. A platform bed built to the room uses that space.

We make platform beds to the room you have. We are a direct workshop: we measure on site, draw the bed, cut each part to the drawing on our own CNC machine in our own Woodlands workshop, and install it ourselves. No middleman, and nothing subcontracted. If you are looking for a platform bed in Singapore that suits a tight room, we can build it to the millimetre.

What we make

Tell us how you sleep and what you need to store, and we will suggest the right type.

  • Platform beds: A low frame with a solid base for the mattress to sit on. Clean lines and no box spring.

  • Storage beds with drawers: Drawers built into the base for bedding, clothes and shoes. We size them to open fully in your room.

  • Lift-up storage beds: The base opens upward on gas lifts, giving you one large space underneath. Suitable for bulky items such as suitcases.

  • Beds with a headboard: A headboard panel with shelves, a ledge, lighting or built-in bedside tables. We cut it to the width of your wall.

  • Beds with bedside units: Side tables and wall-hung shelves built to match, so the whole bed wall reads as one piece.

How we make sure the bed fits

A bed has to fit three things at once: the mattress, the room and the way the room is used. Get one wrong, and you notice every night.

This is what we check.

  • The mattress size: Mattresses are sold in standard sizes, but real mattresses vary by a few millimetres and by thickness. We build to your actual mattress and ask for its specification sheet.

  • Walls that are not square: In older HDB flats, the corners may be out of square. We measure the wall at several points and scribe the bed to it, so the side panel sits flush with no gap.

  • Walking space: We draw the bed in the room at scale, so you can see how much space is left to move, open the door and open the wardrobe.

  • Drawers and lids: We check how far each drawer pulls out and how high the lid lifts, so nothing hits the wall, the window sill or a nearby cabinet.

  • Sockets and switches: If the bedside wall has power points, we cut openings around them, so they stay reachable.

Materials, humidity and a good night's sleep

Humidity in Singapore is typically 70 to 90 per cent. A bed base sits close to the floor and holds a mattress that breathes out moisture, so ventilation matters. We can design the base with slats or vents so air can move under the mattress.

Plywood and particle board are the two common choices. Plywood is generally stronger and handles damp better, while particle board costs less and suits drawers and panels that do not carry weight. We explain the trade-off, and the board, edging and finish are written in your quote.

Edge banding seals the cut edges of the board, which is where moisture first gets in. Drawers get soft-close runners if you want them, so they close quietly at night.

From measurement to installation

Five steps from your first message to a finished bed.

  1. Send a photo: You message us on WhatsApp with a photo of the room, the mattress size and what you want to store.

  2. Site measurement: We visit, measure the room, the walls, the window and any sockets, and check your mattress.

  3. Drawing and quote: We draw the bed to scale in your room and send the drawing with a written quote. You tell us what to change.

  4. Cutting in our own workshop: Each part is cut to the signed drawing on our own CNC machine in Woodlands, so the cuts match the measurements. Nothing is sent out.

  5. Installation: We assemble and level the bed in the room, then check every drawer, lid and gap with you before we leave.

What to prepare before we measure

A few things make the visit quick.

  • Choose the mattress: We need its size, its thickness and, if possible, the specification sheet.

  • Finish the wet works and painting: If the room is being renovated, tell us. We prefer to measure after the walls and floors are done.

  • Clear the floor and wall: Move the old bed and loose furniture so we can reach the corners.

  • Decide what goes underneath: Tell us what you plan to store, so we can size the drawers or lift-up space.

  • Note the position of the wardrobe and the door: We will draw the swing of each, so nothing clashes.

Common questions
Can you build a platform bed for any mattress size?

Yes. We build to your actual mattress, so tell us the size and thickness, or send the specification sheet.

Are storage beds better than a normal bed frame?

In a small bedroom they usually are, because the space under the bed is used. We size the drawers or lift-up base to fit what you want to store.

Will the bed fit in my old HDB flat where the walls are not straight?

We measure the wall at several points and shape the side panels to match, so there is no visible gap against an uneven wall.

Does a platform bed suit Singapore's humid weather?

It can, if the base lets air move and the board edges are sealed. We explain the board and edging choices when we quote.

Do you install the bed?

Yes. We build in our Woodlands workshop and install on site, then check the drawers and gaps with you.
