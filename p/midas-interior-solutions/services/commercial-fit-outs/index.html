Commercial fit-outs

Business

Carpentry for business spaces, fitted to the drawing

When a clinic, shop or office is being fitted out, every day of delay costs money. A cabinet that does not fit means a second visit, a closed room and a missed opening date. Business owners worry about downtime more than anything else.

We make commercial carpentry to the drawing, as a direct workshop. We measure on site, draw each piece, cut every part on our own CNC machine in our own Woodlands workshop, and install it ourselves. There is no subcontractor in the chain and no middleman on the price, so one team is answerable for the whole job. Our aim is to get each piece right the first time, so the space can open on schedule.

What we make for businesses

Our commercial work covers a wide range. These are the jobs we are asked for most.

  • Clinic and aesthetic clinic cabinets: Tall storage cabinets, drawers, built-in vanities with basins and treatment room joinery. Our own clinic project uses oak-laminate cabinets with grey-green drawers and dark frames.

  • Capsule pods: Repeat units with arched niches, steel frames and LED strips. We built pods with arched oak niches and teal steel frames.

  • Reception counters: Straight or curved counters, including a curved stone-effect counter we built for a clinic reception, with the corridor bench and planter beside it.

  • Retail displays and feature walls: Backlit panels, oak and glass shelving and showroom walls. We built a retail showroom feature wall with a backlit panel and oak and glass shelving.

  • Office cabinets: Storage walls, pantry cabinets, filing units and partitions cut to the floor plan.

  • Lounges and waiting areas: Full-height wall panelling and screens, such as the oak panelling and black perforated circular-pattern screen in our lounge project.

How we reduce downtime and re-visits

On a commercial job, most delays come from parts that do not fit and from work that has to be redone. Both start with the measurement.

Here is how we deal with that.

  • Measure after the structure is final: Walls, floors and ceilings move during a fit-out. We measure after plastering, tiling and wet works are done, so the numbers are final.

  • Draw everything first: We draw each piece to scale, including cut-outs for sockets, lights, plumbing and equipment. You approve the drawing before we cut.

  • Cut to the drawing: Our CNC machine cuts every part to the drawing, which gives us exact curves and arches and parts that match from one unit to the next.

  • Install in a planned sequence: We agree the order of work with your contractor, so that carpentry goes in when the area is ready and does not block other trades.

  • Check at handover: We walk through the finished work with you, checking doors, drawers, gaps and fixings.

Repeat units and curved shapes

Capsule pods and clinic rooms are often repeated several times. If each one is cut by hand, small differences add up. When parts are cut from the same digital drawing, each unit matches the next.

Curves are the other test. An arched niche or a curved reception counter cannot be trimmed on site without leaving a mark. We cut the shape to the drawing on our CNC machine, and then check the fit against the wall and floor before installation. If you need a curve or arch, send us a photo of what you have in mind.

Materials for commercial use

Business furniture gets used more than home furniture. Doors open hundreds of times a day, counters take knocks and surfaces are cleaned often. We choose boards, edging and finishes with that in mind.

Singapore humidity is typically 70 to 90 per cent. Air-conditioned rooms and wet areas, such as clinic basins or pantry sinks, need extra care, because moisture gets in at cut edges and holes. We seal these places. We also explain the choice between plywood and particle board, laminate and veneer, and the hardware, such as soft-close hinges. Your quote states the materials, edging and finish in writing.

From measurement to installation

Five steps. For larger jobs, we agree the dates with you and your main contractor.

  1. Send a photo and the plan: You message us on WhatsApp with photos, the floor plan or the design from your designer.

  2. Site measurement: We visit and measure the space, the wall condition, the services and the access route for delivery.

  3. Drawing and quote: We draw each piece and send the drawings with a written quote. You approve the drawings.

  4. Cutting in our own workshop: Each part is cut to the approved drawing on our own CNC machine in Woodlands. Nothing is sent out to another factory.

  5. Installation and handover: We install on the agreed dates and walk through the finished work with you.

What to prepare before we measure

A few answers help us plan one clean site visit.

  • The floor plan or designer's drawing: This shows us what you expect, even if the sizes will change after we measure.

  • Equipment and fittings: Tell us about sinks, basins, screens, lights and any equipment that must fit in or on the carpentry.

  • Site access: Tell us about lift size, loading hours, building rules and whether we need a permit to deliver.

  • Wet works and electrical: Let us know when other trades finish, so we measure at the right time.

  • Your opening date: This lets us tell you what is realistic, and we confirm the schedule in writing after we measure.

Common questions
Do you do retail fit out carpentry in Singapore?

Yes. We build feature walls, backlit panels, and shelving for shops and showrooms. We measure on site, cut to the drawing and install.

Can you build office cabinets to fit our floor plan?

Yes. We draw each cabinet to scale against your floor plan and the actual walls, then cut and install it.

Do you work on clinic renovation projects?

Yes. Our work includes an aesthetic clinic with tall cabinets, drawers and a built-in vanity with basin, as well as a clinic reception counter.

How do you keep re-visits to a minimum?

We measure after wet works, draw before we cut and check the work with you at handover. The same team does all three, with nothing subcontracted, so there is no handover where a number can go wrong.

Do you subcontract any part of the job?

No. We cut in our own workshop in Woodlands on our own CNC machine and our own team installs. You deal with one company from the first measurement to the handover.

Can you build curved or arched pieces?

Yes. Our CNC machine cuts arches and curves to the drawing, as in our capsule pod niches and curved reception counter.

What is the timeline for a commercial job?
