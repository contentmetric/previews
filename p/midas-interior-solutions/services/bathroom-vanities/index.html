Bathroom vanities

Bathrooms

A vanity cabinet built around your bathroom

Bathrooms in Singapore are small, and every centimetre counts. A ready-made vanity rarely lines up with your waste pipe, your tile grout lines or the wall beside it. You end up with a gap at the side, a cabinet that sits proud of the wall, or a door that hits the toilet.

We make vanity cabinets to the room you actually have. We are a direct workshop: we measure on site, draw the cabinet, cut each part to the drawing on our own CNC machine in our own Woodlands workshop, and install it ourselves. No middleman, and nothing subcontracted. If you were searching for a vanity cabinet in Singapore that fits the first time, this is how we work.

What we make

Most bathrooms need one of these. If yours needs something else, send us a photo and we will tell you what is possible.

  • Wall-hung vanities: The cabinet floats above the floor, so you can clean underneath and see more tile. We fix it to the wall at a height that suits the basin and the people using it.

  • Floor-standing vanities: A plinth base sits on the floor and hides uneven tiles. Good for storing larger items such as spare towels and cleaning supplies.

  • Vanities with a built-in basin: A top cut to the exact shape of your basin, with the tap hole in the right place. We confirm the basin and tap before we cut.

  • Mirror cabinets and side storage: Cabinets above or beside the vanity so towels, toiletries and medicine have a home that is not the floor.

  • Narrow cabinets for small bathrooms: Slim units for the tight spaces in HDB flats, where a standard cabinet will not go.

How we make sure the vanity fits

A vanity touches more things than any other cabinet in the house. It meets the wall, the floor, the water supply, the waste pipe, the basin and often a mirror and a light. If one of those is off by a few millimetres, you will see it.

Here is how we deal with each one.

  • Pipes and traps: We mark where the waste pipe and the water supply come out of the wall and cut openings in the back of the cabinet to match. The drawer or shelf is shaped around the trap so it still has room to work.

  • Tiled walls: Tiles are never perfectly flat or square. We measure the wall at several heights and scribe the cabinet to it, so the side panel meets the tile without a visible gap.

  • Floors that slope to the drain: Bathroom floors are laid with a fall. We check the level on site and set the cabinet height and plinth so the doors line up and close.

  • The basin cut-out: Our CNC machine cuts the opening to the basin template, so the edges are clean and the basin sits where the drawing says it will.

  • Taps and mirrors: We check the tap height, the splash zone and the mirror position on the drawing before anything is cut.

Humidity and water: what we plan for

Singapore humidity is high, typically 70 to 90 per cent, and a bathroom adds steam and splashes on top. The two common failures are board that swells at the edges and doors that warp. Both start where water finds raw board.

Raw board is exposed at cut edges, at the basin opening and at the holes for pipes. So these are the places we look after most. We seal the edges, treat the cut-outs, and use silicone where the cabinet meets a wet surface. Plywood and particle board behave differently in damp conditions, and we explain the trade-off for your budget when we quote.

Ventilation matters too. A bathroom with a working window or exhaust fan dries out between uses, and the cabinet lasts longer. If your bathroom stays damp for hours, tell us on WhatsApp and we will plan the layout with that in mind.

Your quote states the board, the edging and the finish in writing, so you know what you are buying.

From measurement to installation

Five steps from your first message to a finished vanity.

  1. Send a photo: You message us on WhatsApp with a photo of the bathroom and the size you have in mind. We tell you what we need to see.

  2. Site measurement: We visit, measure the walls, the pipe positions and the floor, and note the basin and tap you plan to use. We prefer to measure after tiling and wet works are done, so the numbers are final.

  3. Drawing and quote: We draw the vanity to scale and send the drawing with a written quote. You check the drawing and tell us what to change.

  4. Cutting in our own workshop: Each part is cut to the signed drawing on our own CNC machine in Woodlands. Nothing is sent out, and this is the reason the cuts match the measurements.

  5. Installation: We install, level and seal the cabinet and fit the basin top. You check the doors, drawers and gaps with us before we leave.

What to prepare before we measure

A little preparation means one visit is enough.

  • Finish the wet works: Tiling, waterproofing and plastering should be done. Measuring before they finish can mean the numbers change.

  • Have the basin and tap ready: We need the actual model or its specification sheet, because basin shapes and tap positions vary.

  • Clear the area: Take out loose items so we can reach the wall, the pipes and the floor.

  • Know who decides: If the bathroom is shared, have the person who will choose the layout there, so we can agree it on the day.

  • Note the door swing: We check that the bathroom door and the toilet still have the clearance they need.

Common questions
Will a custom vanity cope with Singapore humidity?

It can, if the edges and cut-outs are sealed and the board suits a wet room. We explain the board and edging options when we quote, and your quote lists them in writing.

When do you measure for the vanity?

After tiling and wet works are done. Measuring earlier can mean the wall and floor change, and the cabinet no longer fits.

Do I need to buy the basin first?

Yes, or at least choose the model. The cut-out and tap hole depend on the basin, so we need its specification before we draw.

Can you fit a vanity into a very small HDB bathroom?

Often yes. A slim wall-hung unit or a corner design can work where a standard cabinet cannot. Send a photo and the width of the space and we will advise.

Do you install it as well?

Yes. We fabricate in our Woodlands workshop and install on site, including levelling and sealing.

What does the warranty cover?

Your quote states the warranty in writing after we measure and agree the design.
