Carpentry and CNC

Measured once. Cut exactly.

Measured once. Cut exactly.

Midas Interior Solutions is a carpentry business. We fabricate and install custom carpentry: wardrobes, cabinets, feature walls and fit-outs for homes and commercial spaces, including capsule pods and clinics.

We use a CNC machine for accurate measurements and complex designs. We measure your space once, draw it, and cut each part to the drawing, so it fits your wall. The best way to start is a message on WhatsApp.

What do you make?
