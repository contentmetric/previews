Message us

Message us

  • Do I need clay experience?

    No. Our classes are beginner-friendly and suitable for all ages, from age 4 and above.

  • How do I book?

    Send us a WhatsApp message on +60 17-467 7436, or message us on Facebook or Instagram, and book your spot.

  • How much do classes cost?

    Fees depend on the class, so please message us on WhatsApp for the current rates.

  • Where are you?

    Lot S327A & S327B, 2nd floor, 1 Utama Shopping Centre (New Wing), Bandar Utama, 47800 Petaling Jaya, Selangor.

  • What are your opening hours?

    Every day, 10am to 9pm.

  • What is the Teacher Training Course?

    It is for anyone who wants to teach, start a creative career or master a new skill. You learn directly from Master Chris, with personalised coaching and hands-on training across 9+ clay categories.

  • Do you take group bookings and custom orders?

    Yes. We host team-building workshops and accept custom orders such as food replicas and figurines. Clay materials are also available for purchase.

  • Do you have another studio?

    Yes. Our headquarters is here at 1 Utama, and our Yenji Artist Lab studio is at KLGCC Mall.
