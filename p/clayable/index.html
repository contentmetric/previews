Frequently Asked Questions

Answers from the Clayable website. For anything else, send a WhatsApp message.

Still have a question?

Message us

Message us

  • Do I need experience to join a class?

    No prior experience is needed. Our trainers teach the basics first, then build up from there based on how you’re doing in class.

  • How do I book?

    Send a WhatsApp message to +65 8840 6615 with the class you are interested in, or use any Book button on this page.

  • How much do classes cost?

    The featured classes above start at SGD 85. Other classes, pottery painting and group sessions are priced separately, so message us for the one you want.

  • What is the difference between wheel throwing and hand building?

    Wheel throwing uses a spinning wheel to shape round pieces like bowls and cups. Hand building uses simple tools and hand techniques to make pieces with more varied, uneven shapes.

  • Where is the studio and when is it open?

    2 Havelock Rd, #02-12, Singapore 059763. Open daily from 11 am to 5 pm.

  • Can I book a private group or team event?

    Yes. You can book a private session for a corporate team, birthday or other group gathering by contacting our team directly. Birthday parties host up to 20 guests at one time, and corporate sessions run for 5 to 50 people.

  • When will my pottery be ready?

    After your session, finished pieces are fired in a kiln before they are ready to use. We will let you know when your piece is ready for pickup.

  • Do you run other workshops?

    Yes. Besides pottery, Clayable runs a Tea Appreciation Workshop for groups of 10 to 20 and an Art Jamming workshop.
