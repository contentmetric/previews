Questions

Ask us anything.

Is it really cat-only?

Yes. Every guest, every suite and every grooming room is for cats only.

When are you open?

Monday to Thursday 10:00–18:30, Friday 10:00–13:00, Saturday and Sunday 10:00–18:30.

Where are you?

A-03-01 GF & Mezzanine, Worldwide@7, Lot 24, Jalan Lazuardi 7/29, Seksyen 7, 40000 Shah Alam, Selangor.

What does grooming include?

A gentle bath, massage and careful drying, plus nail clipping, eye and nose cleaning, and paw and sanitary trimming.

Can I see my cat while I am away?

Yes — we send daily video and photo updates during the stay, and guests can even ask for a live stream of the grooming session.

How much does it cost?

We do not publish a rate card. Message us on WhatsApp for the current rates.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp at 012-720 4011. Tell us which service you need and the dates, and we will confirm.

Anything else — message us on WhatsApp at 012-720 4011.
