Questions parents ask

Where are the classes held?

At The Midtown: 1187 Upper Serangoon Rd, #02-19, Singapore 534785. Message us on WhatsApp if you need a hand finding the unit.

What do you teach, and from which level?

Primary Science, from Primary 3 to PSLE. Children join from Primary 3 and stay with us to PSLE. Message us on WhatsApp if you would like to ask about your child's level.

What are your class hours?

Mon closed. Tue 6:30–8:15 PM. Wed 4:15–8:15 PM. Thu 4:30–8:15 PM. Fri 4:00–8:15 PM. Sat 9 AM–3:45 PM. Sun 9 AM–3:30 PM. Classes run after school on weekdays, and through the day at the weekend.

How do I ask about fees and places?

Message us on WhatsApp at 9820 5432. Tell us your child's level and we will come back to you on what a place costs and what is still open.

Can I register my child for 2027?

Yes — registration for 2027 has begun. Send us a message on WhatsApp and we will tell you what is open for your child's level.
